If you subscribe to our email brief, we collect and store your email address for the sole purpose of sending you that brief. We do not collect names, payment details, or any other personal information through this site.

Your email address is used only to send the daily market brief. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties, other than our email-sending provider (Resend), which processes it solely to deliver our emails.

Unsubscribing

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Clicking it removes you from future sends.

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics (e.g. aggregate page-view counts) to understand which content is useful. This data is not tied to your identity.
